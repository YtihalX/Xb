# §I computes the X_b2 and the dressed χ_b2 spectrum only

Decided by: you
Date: 2026-10-28
Source: the grilling session of 2026-10-28
Status: the out-of-scope item "$1/m_b$ corrections beyond $V_0^{(2^{++})}$" is superseded by 0004

§I of `followup.tex` computes, in LO BOχEFT with the $1^{++}$ parameters, the $X_{b2}$ pole and the $\chi_{b2}(1P)$-, $\chi_{b2}(2P)$-, $\chi_{b2}(3P)$-dominated states, in the baseline and shallow-$X_b$ fits. Out of scope: the $0^{++}$ members of the multiplet; the isovector $W_{bJ}$; production and radiative decays of the $X_{b2}$; isospin breaking; $1/m_b$ corrections beyond $V_0^{(2^{++})}$; the $S$–$D$ counterterm unless decisions/0002 escalates; bare-state couplings to $D$-wave channels. The charm sector (§II) and the CMS line shapes (§III) are deferred to their own grillings, because §III needs the §I spectrum.
