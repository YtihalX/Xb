## NOTEBOOK.md, Open questions, Path quadrature (S40), paragraph "Near nodes"

  Near nodes (proposed 2026-11-13, not adopted; draft, "Tiling"; figures `notes/07-continued-lse/gap_failures.png` and `s35_h2.png` from `gap_failures.py`, data `gap_failures_fix.jl`; plot evidence, not a check). The two gaps of the tiled search domain lie where the unflipped pole of a channel just below its threshold comes within $r_{\rm near}$ of the path and becomes a node. The only failing pair is the node pair $(k,k)$ with $s_+$ and pion exchange, next to the one-pion-exchange left-hand cut of that channel ($\kappa_5>65.2$ MeV, $\kappa_1>m_\pi/2$): the singular points of $V(k,\cdot)$ that carry the cut lie $2\operatorname{Re}k$ from the node's vertical homotopy (21.6 MeV at $-46.60-0.35i$, 2.5 MeV at $-1.40-0.04i$). At the sampled energies $Z_+$ stays below the cut of the logarithm while $\operatorname{Im}E<0$, but with $\operatorname{Re}Z<0$, which C5 cannot certify; on the axis the pair lies on the cut. The pole lies on the sheet's side of every path with slopes at most 1, so its node only improves accuracy. Proposal: such a channel takes a node only when the node passes C4 and C5; otherwise it stays without one, with its clearance recorded. With the node off ($r_{\rm near}=60$ MeV), all 100 and 15 excluded cells of the two gaps certify, the pole at least 64.1 and 67.1 MeV from the path.
