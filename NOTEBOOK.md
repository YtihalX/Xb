## Question

With the interaction fixed in $1^{++}$ LO BOχEFT, is there a molecule-dominated spin-2 partner $X_{b2}$ near the $B^*\bar B^*$ threshold in the baseline and shallow-$X_b$ fits? Where are its pole $M-i\Gamma/2$ and the $\chi_{b2}(1P)$-, $\chi_{b2}(2P)$-, and $\chi_{b2}(3P)$-dominated states, and how do they compare with the measured peaks? Starting question: `followup.tex` §I; `plan/PLAN.md`, Research question. The charm partners and CMS line shapes remain separate questions.

## Established

- **Baseline $1^{++}$ spectrum.** The $(1P,2P,X_b,3P)$ masses are $(9.893264,10.255276,10.513270,10.543615)$ GeV, reproducing [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Table V within 1 MeV and component probabilities within one percentage point.
  Computation and comparison: `checks/baseline_fit.jl`; physics table: `paper/sections/framework.tex`.
- **Shallow-$X_b$ fit.** With the spurious pole projected out, $E_B[X_b]=1$ MeV, $C=2.6646\ \text{GeV}^{-2}$, $V_0=361.60$ MeV and $\Delta M=14.994$ MeV reproduce Table VIII; masses $(1P,2P,3P,X_b)=(9.8835,10.2453,10.5351,10.6033)$ GeV. Physical masses vary by at most $0.243$ MeV over $\varepsilon=5$–$50$ GeV.
  Results: `checks/shallow_fit.jl`, `checks/spurious_projection.jl`, `checks/shallow_spurious.jl`; table: `paper/sections/framework.tex`.
- **$1^{++}$ OME projection.** All nine entries reproduce [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Eqs. (D2)–(D10), in the printed (D1) phase; independent angular integration agrees. The package's former $^5D_1$ phase differed by $U=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1)$.
  Derivation: `notes/03-projection-method-1pp.md`; comparison: `checks/projection_1pp.py`; exposition: `paper/sections/app-ome-2pp.tex`.
- **Six-channel $2^{++}$ OME.** All 21 independent entries are derived through $\mathcal N^{(m)}_{\alpha'\alpha}\Delta_m^{(\beta_1\beta_2)}$, with moments through $m=4$. The $B\bar B\to B^*\bar B^*$ term has the positive sign and $2D_3$ of `followup.tex`, with $2D_4$ in reverse; angular integration, time reversal and the strict-HQSS $J_\ell=1$ block confirm the potential.
  Derivation: `notes/04-ome-2pp.md`; checks: `checks/projection_2pp.py`, `checks/time_reversal_2pp.jl`, `checks/hqss_2pp.py`, `checks/projected_spin_numerator_2pp.py`; formulas: `paper/sections/app-ome-2pp.tex`.
- **Charm width toy.** The bare Cornell $2P$ golden-rule estimate gives $\Gamma[\chi_{c1}(4010)\to D\bar D^*]=13.9$ MeV ($11$–$17$ MeV over the mass uncertainty), versus measured $62.7$ MeV. The physical $p=519$ MeV lies near the $\psi_2$ node at $439$ MeV; an independent radial solve and the nonrelativistic $14.4$ MeV estimate confirm this limited calculation, which omits dressing, OME and contact interactions.
  Model and assumptions: `toy/chi_c1_4010_width/run.jl`, `toy/chi_c1_4010_width/README.md`; analysis: `briefs/2026-10-29-chi-c1-4010-width.md`.

## Open questions

- **$X_{b2}$ position.** Not known yet in either fit: existence, $M$, $\Gamma$, binding energy relative to $2m_{B^*}$, sheet and composition remain undetermined. An HQSS-limit estimate is not a physical pole prediction (`plan/PLAN.md`).
- **Bare $\chi_{b2}$ masses.** Choose the unchanged $1^{++}$ bare masses or spin-dependent shifts from [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Eq. (B4); settle the role of $\chi_{b2}(9912)$ and $\chi_{b2}(10269)$ and which states the spin-dependent calculation covers. Because $V_0$ already absorbs the $1^{++}$ shift, adding the full $2^{++}$ shift risks double counting. The dressed spectrum, $X_{b2}$ prediction and CMS comparison depend on this choice (`decisions/0004-quark-model-fixed.md`; `plan/PLAN.md`).
- **Renormalisation and uncertainty.** Does $C$ alone give a stable $2^{++}$ prediction across $\Lambda=3$–$5$ GeV? The shallow-$X_b$ fit still needs $1^{++}$ refits at $3,5$ GeV. Tower, projection-strength and grid dependence remain to be determined for $2^{++}$; a change of pole nature reopens the counterterm question (`decisions/0002-2pp-renormalisation.md`; `plan/PLAN.md`).
- **Contour definition needs your decision.** The agreed wording in `CONVENTIONS.md`, “Continuation to other sheets”, excludes every contour once $|\operatorname{Im}k_\alpha|>m_\phi$: a kernel branch point then lies between the real axis and the contour. The proposed replacement criterion is that no singularity crosses $\mathcal C$ during deformation; it awaits your OK (`plan/slices/05-continuation-kernel.md`, Paused).
- **Branch discrepancy.** Scratch probes recorded 62–134 disagreements out of 6144 pion contour pairs between the continuity tracker and principal split-log moments at depth $0.2$ GeV. The ratio-log route disagrees at about as many pairs. Cause not known: homotopy crossing, tracker step control and contour admissibility remain candidates; resolve this before choosing a correction (`plan/slices/05-continuation-kernel.md`, Paused).
- **Tolerances need your decision.** The $10^{-6}$ relative target for complex $f_n$ is not reached over the full stated domain: recorded matched-tail differences are about $10^{-6}$–$4\times10^{-6}$ on the refined coordinate grid and $7.6\times10^{-6}$ on the production grid. Draft checks use $5\times10^{-6}$ and $10^{-5}$; these are proposals, not approved replacements. The later directions retain targets of $0.1$ MeV for poles, $10^{-6}$ relative for on-shell $T$, and next-order estimates for the ERE and $g^2$ comparisons (`plan/slices/05-continuation-kernel.md`; `plan/slices/06-continued-lse.md`; `plan/slices/07-continuation-poles.md`).
- **Kernel continuation.** The App. D prototype is recorded, but the continued TOPT moments, direct angular comparison, three-body locator, left-hand-cut maps and complex form factors are not established project results. Existing drafts: `checks/appd_contours.py`, `checks/three_body_locator.jl`, `checks/complex_form_factors.jl`, `checks/lhc_1pp.jl`, `checks/lhc_scan_2pp.jl` (`plan/slices/05-continuation-kernel.md`, Paused).
- **Continued LSE: possible direction.** How to solve on finite contours with channel-by-channel sheet signs, recover the real-axis amplitude and unitarity, reproduce the baseline bound states, and establish contour independence? Candidate independent routes are the on-shell-point method for $|\operatorname{Im}k_\alpha|<m_\pi$ and an exactly soluble two-channel model. The requested contour-deformation figures remain open; virtual states may need a short negative-$\operatorname{Re}q$ excursion with $\kappa_v<m_\pi/2$ (`plan/slices/06-continued-lse.md`).
- **Poles and couplings: possible direction.** Pole counting by the argument principle, refinement, narrow test resonances, a $g$-scan into the broad regime, a virtual $X_b$, and resonance residues remain to be investigated. No broad test pole with OME is established yet. Candidate comparisons are a real-axis speed plot, the $O(g^2)$ self-energy width, the effective-range expansion, and $\sum_\alpha\Gamma_\alpha=\Gamma$ for a narrow resonance; the user selects the checks and their criteria (`plan/slices/07-continuation-poles.md`).
- **Phenomenology.** Which predicted state, if any, can explain $\chi_{b2}(10524)$, and how does $X_{b2}$ compare with published predictions? These require the spectrum first; the CMS mass ordering is not a spin measurement (`plan/PLAN.md`).
- **Deferred charm and line shapes.** Does a dressed $\chi_{c1}(2P)$ pole explain $\chi_{c1}(4010)$ despite the toy's small width and node sensitivity? The charm calculation needs the three-body cut; `followup.tex`'s “imaginary part around 60 MeV” needs clarification as $\Gamma$ versus $-\operatorname{Im}E_{\rm pole}$. $X(3872)$, $X_2$ and CMS line-shape analysis await their own user choices (`followup.tex`; `briefs/2026-10-29-chi-c1-4010-width.md`; `plan/PLAN.md`).
