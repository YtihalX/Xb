## Question

With the interaction fixed in $1^{++}$ LO BOχEFT, is there a molecule-dominated spin-2 partner $X_{b2}$ near the $B^*\bar B^*$ threshold in the baseline and shallow-$X_b$ fits? Where are its pole $M-i\Gamma/2$ and the $\chi_{b2}(1P)$-, $\chi_{b2}(2P)$-, and $\chi_{b2}(3P)$-dominated states, and how do they compare with the measured peaks? Starting question: `followup.tex` §I. The charm partners and CMS line shapes remain separate questions.

## Established

- **Baseline $1^{++}$ spectrum.** The $(1P,2P,X_b,3P)$ masses are $(9.893264,10.255276,10.513270,10.543615)$ GeV, reproducing [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Table V within 1 MeV and component probabilities within one percentage point.
  Computation and comparison: `checks/baseline_fit.jl`; physics table: `paper/sections/framework.tex`.
- **Shallow-$X_b$ fit.** With the spurious pole projected out, $E_B[X_b]=1$ MeV, $C=2.6646\ \text{GeV}^{-2}$, $V_0=361.60$ MeV and $\Delta M=14.994$ MeV reproduce Table VIII; masses $(1P,2P,3P,X_b)=(9.8835,10.2453,10.5351,10.6033)$ GeV. Physical masses vary by at most $0.243$ MeV over $\varepsilon=5$–$50$ GeV.
  Results: `checks/shallow_fit.jl`, `checks/spurious_projection.jl`, `checks/shallow_spurious.jl`; table: `paper/sections/framework.tex`.
- **$1^{++}$ OME projection.** All nine entries reproduce [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Eqs. (D2)–(D10), in the printed (D1) phase; independent angular integration agrees.
  Derivation: `notes/03-projection-method-1pp.md`; comparison: `checks/projection_1pp.py`; exposition: `paper/sections/app-ome-2pp.tex`.
- **Six-channel $2^{++}$ OME.** All 21 independent entries are derived through $\mathcal N^{(m)}_{\alpha'\alpha}\Delta_m^{(\beta_1\beta_2)}$, with moments through $m=4$. The $B\bar B\to B^*\bar B^*$ term has the positive sign and $2D_3$ of `followup.tex`, with $2D_4$ in reverse; angular integration, time reversal and the strict-HQSS $J_\ell=1$ block confirm the potential.
  Derivation: `notes/04-ome-2pp.md`; checks: `checks/projection_2pp.py`, `checks/time_reversal_2pp.jl`, `checks/hqss_2pp.py`, `checks/projected_spin_numerator_2pp.py`; formulas: `paper/sections/app-ome-2pp.tex`.
- **Charm width toy.** The bare Cornell $2P$ golden-rule estimate gives $\Gamma[\chi_{c1}(4010)\to D\bar D^*]=13.9$ MeV ($11$–$17$ MeV over the mass uncertainty), versus measured $62.7$ MeV. The physical $p=519$ MeV lies near the $\psi_2$ node at $439$ MeV; an independent radial solve and the nonrelativistic $14.4$ MeV estimate confirm this limited calculation, which omits dressing, OME and contact interactions. Over $M=4012.5\pm10$ MeV the width runs $8.5$–$19.7$ MeV, $4.1\%$ per MeV at the centre against $0.35\%$ per MeV from phase space alone, and vanishes where $p$ reaches the node, at $M=3974.5$ MeV; the radial solve reproduces $\psi_2$, $\Gamma$, the node and the slope across this band to the printed digits.
  Model and assumptions: `toy/chi_c1_4010_width/run.jl`, `toy/chi_c1_4010_width/README.md`; mass band: `toy/chi_c1_4010_width/plot.jl`, check `checks/chi_c1_4010_band.jl`.

## Decisions

- **LO contact term** (2026-10-28; user). The $1^{++}$ contact $C$ alone acts in $^5S_2$: no $2^{++}$ data fix an additional $S$–$D$ term. The cutoff spread is quoted as LO uncertainty; if the pole crosses a threshold, changes sheet or merges with the $\chi_{b2}(3P)$-dominated state, the prediction counts as unrenormalised and this choice reopens. A spurious pole above the lowest compact-dominated state is projected out; otherwise it is kept and its projection shift reported.
- **§I scope** (2026-10-28; user). $X_{b2}$ and the dressed $\chi_{b2}(1P,2P,3P)$ spectrum in both fits. Charm and the CMS line shapes come later; the line shapes need that spectrum. Outside this question: $0^{++}$, isovectors, production, radiative decays, isospin breaking, bare-state $D$-wave couplings.
- **Quark model fixed** (2026-10-28; colleague, adopted by the user). The quark model $(m_b,\sigma,\alpha_S,V_0)$ is fixed once for all quantum numbers, with the previously fitted parameters ($\gamma=-\tfrac43\alpha_s$); nothing is refitted in $2^{++}$. The colleague suggested computing the spin-dependent spectrum from the paper's formulas; whether and how far to do so is open (Bare $\chi_{b2}$ masses).
- **$^5D_1$ phase** (2026-10-29; user). The package follows the printed phase of [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Eq. (D1); direct angular integration and the printed projected formulas agree with it.
- **Spurious-pole identification** (2026-10-29; user). The spurious pole is the predominantly hadronic member through the avoided crossing.
- **Projection precision** (2026-10-29; user). Projection-strength stability is judged at the paper's 1 MeV precision; the baseline $2P$ spread is $0.104$ MeV.
- **Continuation route** (2026-10-30; user, grilling of 2026-10-29/30). Off-physical-sheet poles are reached on a deformed finite loop contour; the on-shell-point route serves as a second route only for $|\operatorname{Im}k_\alpha|<m_\pi$. The sharp cutoff excludes scaling to infinity; keeping the real axis beyond that domain needs explicit cut terms. Branches are tracked with split logs and an independent continuity tracker; complex form factors are bilinear. On-shell bound-state couplings are out of scope, because the Table V poles lie on the OME left-hand cut. The three-body locator stays for later charm work, without charm dynamics. An obstruction to a target contour goes back to the user.
- **Singularity map** (2026-10-30; user). The map covers thresholds, left- and right-hand cuts in each channel's complex momentum plane, and the singularities of the LSE integrand. It assumes no pole positions: $X_{b2}$ is not yet determined. The later contour figure uses the same planes.

## Open questions

- **$X_{b2}$ position.** Not known yet in either fit: existence, $M$, $\Gamma$, binding energy relative to $2m_{B^*}$, sheet and composition remain undetermined. An HQSS-limit estimate is not a physical pole prediction.
- **Bare $\chi_{b2}$ masses.** Choose the unchanged $1^{++}$ bare masses or spin-dependent shifts from [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1, Eq. (B4); settle the role of $\chi_{b2}(9912)$ and $\chi_{b2}(10269)$ and which states the spin-dependent calculation covers. Because $V_0$ already absorbs the $1^{++}$ shift, adding the full $2^{++}$ shift risks double counting. The dressed spectrum, $X_{b2}$ prediction and CMS comparison depend on this choice.
- **Renormalisation and uncertainty.** Does $C$ alone give a stable $2^{++}$ prediction across $\Lambda=3$–$5$ GeV? The shallow-$X_b$ fit still needs $1^{++}$ refits at $3,5$ GeV. Tower, projection-strength and grid dependence remain to be determined for $2^{++}$; a change of pole nature reopens the counterterm question.
- **Contour definition needs your decision.** The agreed wording in `CONVENTIONS.md`, “Continuation to other sheets”, excludes every contour once $|\operatorname{Im}k_\alpha|>m_\phi$: a kernel branch point then lies between the real axis and the contour. The proposed replacement criterion is that no singularity crosses $\mathcal C$ during deformation; it awaits your OK.
- **Branch discrepancy.** Scratch probes recorded 62–134 disagreements out of 6144 pion contour pairs between the continuity tracker and principal split-log moments at depth $0.2$ GeV. The ratio-log route disagrees at about as many pairs. Cause not known: homotopy crossing, tracker step control and contour admissibility remain candidates; resolve this before choosing a correction.
- **Ratio-log representation ([@Wu:2026wxb], App. D).** For which admissible App. D contours does the principal ratio log differ from continuously tracked split logs, and how does this extend from the static Yukawa example to the full TOPT kernel? Establish the contour and branch prescriptions before claiming a failing case.
- **Tolerances need your decision.** The $10^{-6}$ relative target for complex $f_n$ is not reached over the full stated domain: matched-tail differences are about $10^{-6}$–$4\times10^{-6}$ on the refined coordinate grid and $7.6\times10^{-6}$ on the production grid. Draft checks use $5\times10^{-6}$ and $10^{-5}$; these are proposals, not approved replacements. The continuation directions below keep targets of $0.1$ MeV for poles, $10^{-6}$ relative for on-shell $T$, and next-order estimates for the ERE and $g^2$ comparisons.
- **Kernel continuation.** The continued TOPT moments, direct angular comparison, three-body locator, left-hand-cut maps and complex form factors are not yet established. Drafts: `checks/appd_contours.py` (App. D contour prototype), `checks/three_body_locator.jl`, `checks/complex_form_factors.jl`, `checks/lhc_1pp.jl`, `checks/lhc_scan_2pp.jl`.
- **Continued LSE: possible direction.** How to solve on finite contours with channel-by-channel sheet signs, recover the real-axis amplitude and unitarity, reproduce the baseline bound states, and establish contour independence? Candidate independent routes are the on-shell-point method for $|\operatorname{Im}k_\alpha|<m_\pi$ and an exactly soluble two-channel model. The requested contour-deformation figures remain open; virtual states may need a short negative-$\operatorname{Re}q$ excursion with $\kappa_v<m_\pi/2$.
- **Poles and couplings: possible direction.** Pole counting by the argument principle, refinement, narrow test resonances, a $g$-scan into the broad regime, a virtual $X_b$, and resonance residues remain to be investigated. No broad test pole with OME is established yet. Candidate comparisons are a real-axis speed plot, the $O(g^2)$ self-energy width, the effective-range expansion, and $\sum_\alpha\Gamma_\alpha=\Gamma$ for a narrow resonance; the user selects the checks and their criteria.
- **Phenomenology.** Which predicted state, if any, can explain $\chi_{b2}(10524)$, and how does $X_{b2}$ compare with published predictions? These require the spectrum first; the CMS mass ordering is not a spin measurement.
- **Deferred charm and line shapes.** Does a dressed $\chi_{c1}(2P)$ pole explain $\chi_{c1}(4010)$ despite the toy's small width and node sensitivity? With $M$ fixed at $4012.5$ MeV and only $p$ varied, the toy's width peaks at $52.7$ MeV beyond the node ($p=701.8$ MeV) and at $135.9$ MeV below it ($p=159.7$ MeV); not yet checked (`toy/chi_c1_4010_width/momentum_scan.jl`). The charm calculation needs the three-body cut; `followup.tex`'s “imaginary part around 60 MeV” needs clarification as $\Gamma$ versus $-\operatorname{Im}E_{\rm pole}$. $X(3872)$, $X_2$ and CMS line-shape analysis await their own user choices.
