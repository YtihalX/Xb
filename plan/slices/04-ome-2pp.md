## Result

The projected $2^{++}$ OME is $V^{\rm(OME)}_{\alpha'\alpha}=-\frac{g_b^2}{8F_\pi^2}\sum_{k=0}^4c_{\alpha'\alpha,k}(p',p)\,[I_k^{(\beta)}+I_k^{(\gamma)}]$ for all 21 independent entries of the six-channel basis. The new $B\bar B\leftrightarrow B^*\bar B^*$ transition is $+\frac{g_b^2}{4F_\pi^2}(\boldsymbol\epsilon_1'^*\cdot\boldsymbol q)(\boldsymbol\epsilon_2'^*\cdot\boldsymbol q)\,2D_3$, with $2D_4$ for the reverse. Its sign follows from Eq. (68) relative to Eq. (72) of [@Zhang:2026iyr] v1 in the canonical $C$ convention, so followup.tex Eq. (5) is right, sign included; "B^*\bar B^8" is a typo. The $G$-wave entries need moments up to $x^4$. Four upper-triangle entries vanish identically: ${}^5S_2$–${}^5G_2$, ${}^1D_2$–$B\bar B^*({}^3D_2)$, and the two $B\bar B$ entries without a $B^*\bar B^*$ leg. The package agrees with brute-force Eq. (80) to $2.9\times10^{-12}$ GeV$^{-2}$. It is reciprocal to $7\times10^{-16}$ GeV$^{-2}$. In strict HQSS it is block diagonal in $(s_H,j_\ell)$ to $10^{-15}$, and its $J_\ell=1$ block equals the $1^{++}$ one to $10^{-13}$ GeV$^{-2}$. One species-based kernel now serves $1^{++}$ and $2^{++}$, and every $1^{++}$ frozen-reference comparison stays bit-identical.
